## Privacy And Permissions Notes

Required permissions:
- Microphone: needed for speech detection, voice translation, and live subtitles
- Camera: needed for video calls
- Network access: needed for WebRTC, signaling, translation, and subscription status

Privacy policy URL:

https://bridgecall.tech/privacy

Privacy policy must mention:
- Microphone access
- Voice/audio processing
- Live translation processing
- Subscription and purchase data handled through RevenueCat
- Device and technical diagnostic data
- Error logs
- Data retention and deletion contact

## App Review Checklist

- Privacy Policy URL is added in App Store Connect
- Privacy Policy link is visible in the app Settings/About area
- RevenueCat subscription information is disclosed in privacy policy
- Microphone and camera permission strings are clear and user-facing
- Demo account or demo instructions are available if Apple needs to test calls
- Subscription products are configured and approved
- Restore purchase button is visible
- Free usage limits are clear before paywall
- App screenshots show onboarding, call screen, live subtitle, paywall, and settings
- No fake social proof is shown unless backed by real production metrics
